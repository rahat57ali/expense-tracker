# Ledgr

A privacy-first personal finance app for Android. Track expenses, 
manage budgets, monitor bills, and understand your spending — with 
all data stored locally on your device.

No ads. No tracking. No cloud.

## Features

- Expense tracking with custom and predefined categories
- Monthly budget planning with per-category allocation
- Income source tracking including rollover amounts
- Grocery lists with receipt photos and expense conversion
- Voice memos for on-the-go expense reminders
- Recurring bills with smart due date handling
- Deep monthly analytics and personalized spending tips
- Month-end rollover wizard with dynamic insights
- XLSX export and CSV/XLSX import with duplicate detection
- Dark and light themes with OLED-optimized dark mode
- Local-first storage — all data stays on device
